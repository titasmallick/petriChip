📖 SYSTEM ARCHIVE: COMPREHENSIVE BIOLOGICAL PHENOMENA

You are observing a high-speed Artificial Life simulation running entirely on Node.js. Every triangle is an independent agent powered by its own mutating Neural Network. There is no central artificial objective—they must mathematically learn to survive. Here are the precise evolutionary dynamics playing out live:

  • Natural Selection & Poison Avoidance: Cyan dots (+60 energy) are food. Red dots (-80 energy) are poison. Early generations have random brain wirings and will eat poison and die. Only those that randomly mutate a "Turn Away From Poison" reflex survive.
  • Bacterial Toxicity: Dead organisms lyse and release toxic metabolic waste. When an organism dies, its corpse leaves a Red Poison dot, permanently polluting the environment.
  • Evolvable Morphology (r/K Strategy): Organisms evolve bodies, not just brains. They have genes for Speed, Vision, and Body Size. Size dictates life strategy: massive organisms (K-selection) have high predation power but move slower and burn massive calories. Tiny organisms (r-selection) are weak but breed rapidly on scraps. This often leads to Insular Dwarfism (shrinking to conserve energy).
  • The Red Queen Hypothesis (Arms Race): As food becomes scarce, the agents must evolve to be faster (Avg Speed) just to beat their siblings to the remaining food. They are forced to run at maximum speed simply to avoid extinction.
  • Rapid Encephalization: The environment's penalty for stupidity (eating poison) is so lethal that the survival benefit of a highly complex brain vastly outweighs its high metabolic cost, leading to rapid brain growth (Avg Connects).
  • Senescence (Aging) & Mortality: Organisms are not immortal. As they age, their metabolic efficiency decays, forcing them to hunt faster before time runs out. There is also a constant stochastic mortality rate (random death via disease/accidents).
  • Speciation via Assortative Mating: When two organisms of a similar genetic color (Kin) bump into each other, they mate, spawning a child that inherits a genetic crossover. Mating costs 40 energy. They practice Mate Choice, refusing to mate with starving runts or vastly differently sized organisms. Over time, they naturally segregate into distinct, color-coded species.
  • Mitosis (Asexual Reproduction): An organism can clone itself asexually, but it is brutally expensive (cost: 120 energy). They must hoard massive amounts of food to survive the process.
  • Asymmetric Predation: When different species (alien colors) collide, the stronger organism—calculated by multiplying its energy by its evolved Speed and Body Size—physically overpowers the weaker species and steals its energy.
  • True Pathogens & Immunology: Winter triggers viral outbreaks. Viruses drain energy and spread on contact (green glowing aura). Survival requires evolving a massive Immune System (gene_immunity), but maintaining high immunity costs permanent passive energy, triggering an auto-immune calorie trap.
  • Biogeochemical Nutrient Cycling: There are no more infinite food spawns. Food requires Fertilizer in the soil. The environment demands Trophic Levels:
    • 🌱 Producers: Can evolve gene_chloroplast to passively generate energy from the Sun, but it makes them heavy and completely immobile.
    • 🥩 Carnivores: Can evolve gene_carnivore to steal massive energy from hunting others, but it permanently breaks their ability to digest plant matter.
    • 🍄 Decomposers (Scavengers): Corpses are toxic poison to most, but creatures can evolve gene_scavenger to digest the dead. Eating corpses is the ONLY way to return Fertilizer back to the global soil to spawn more plants, closing the nutrient loop!
