
## Version 2: The Ecological & Epidemiological Update
We have introduced \server_v2.js\ and \public/index_v2.html\ which massively expand the simulation by introducing:
- **Trophic Levels & Biogeochemical Cycling:** Plants, Herbivores, Carnivores, and Scavengers mathematically balance a global Fertilizer supply.
- **Epidemiology:** Winter triggers viral pathogens. The population must evolve immunity, balancing the high metabolic cost of an immune system against the risk of infection.
- **Seasons (Climate Change):** Dynamic rotations between Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter, shifting the optimal r/K reproductive strategy on the fly.
- **Shape-Shifting Morphologies:** Organisms visually evolve distinct shapes based on their dominant Trophic Gene (Circles = Plants, Spikes = Carnivores, Squares = Scavengers, Triangles = Herbivores).
